# GGA0401 — Itha-Yen

Experimental Seraphim hover assault tank, added 22 October 2026 to
`seraphim-rework`. No other mod is required. Enable **Seraphim Rework** and build
with a Seraphim T3 engineer or commander with T3 engineering. Blueprint ID for
the sandbox unit-spawn menu: `gga0401`.

The main weapon is `UWRC_DirectFire`, `Direct Fire Experimental`, with
`RULEUBA_None` and a `DIRECTFIRE` projectile. It has no artillery arc or missile
tracking. Five seconds of projectile lifetime allow 175 units of unobstructed
travel at speed 35, exceeding the current 80 range. Terrain can block it;
vision is 50, so firing to maximum range requires spotting.

The shield uses `PersonalShield = true`, not a spherical collision shield. FAF's
`lua/shield.lua` disables its shield-entity collision, while `lua/sim/Unit.lua`
routes damage to the owner's shield. `OwnerShieldMesh` points to the tank's own
normal mesh because the native personal-shield implementation replaces the
owner mesh when enabled. No dome or white shield coating is used. Shield bars,
toggle and hit effects use standard FAF behavior. Shield and weapon behavior
still need in-game verification.

## Model and regeneration

The original geometry is generated by `generate_hover_tank.py`; the adjacent
`tank_geometry.py` and `supcom_binary.py` are self-contained project-owned
primitives, renderer and SCM/BC3 writer adapted from GGA0101. They do not import
another unit's sources. No stock model or texture has been copied into this
unit. Stock sounds, particle effects, AA projectiles and Lua classes are
referenced from the game.

```powershell
$tankPython = 'C:\Users\Nestr\.cache\codex-runtimes\codex-primary-runtime\dependencies\python\python.exe'
& $tankPython 'mods\seraphim-rework\source-assets\GGA0401\generate_hover_tank.py'
```

Requires Python, numpy and Pillow. Source axes are Y up and +Z forward;
`UniformScale = 0.1`. Neutral-pose bounds from the exported SCM are
**3.962×1.690×5.90** game units (LOD0, width × occupied height × length).
The model spans Y=0.275–1.965, and hover elevation is another 0.4. The geometry
uses a swept central keel, unequal separate hull islands, rolled rims, three
open fins, an open twin-jaw cannon, a shield core and four AA turrets.
Each turret has separate yaw/pitch/muzzle
bones. The main muzzle is at source `(0,15,25)` before aiming, just beyond the
focusing jaws. No decorative animation controls weapon bones. The main pitch
arc is limited to -10° through +30° to avoid aiming the long barrel through the
ground. Exact engine aiming conventions and clearance require match testing.

### Naval hull rebuild — 22 October 2026

The user's ship screenshot is preserved as `reference-seraphim-ship.png`.
It guides the long, swept metallic plates, open upright fins, asymmetric outline
and detached, jointly levitating hull sections. The hull is an interpretation
within the tank's existing 4×6 size, rather than a copy of a stock ship mesh.
Stock XSS0302/XSS0201 blueprint shader settings and the XSS0302 SCM skeleton were
read locally for comparison. This land unit retains its native `Seraphim`
material setup; no ship model, texture, navy shader or naval movement is copied.

The old broad base and paired continuous pods are replaced by a narrow curved
central keel, one long port island, separate starboard fore/aft islands, and an
offset aft dorsal shard. Exterior canards and three unequal open fins reinforce
the naval silhouette. Gaps between the main hull islands are genuine empty space;
there is no common connecting slab underneath. Cambered profiles and rolled
edges are modeled with closed smoothed geometry, using Catmull-Rom sections.

Four new bones (`PortHull`, `StarboardFore`, `StarboardAft`, `AftShard`) carry
the separated pieces. After construction, the unit script gives them an
8-second vertical drift with different phases and 0.02–0.03 game-unit amplitude.
AA1/AA3 are parented to the port hull, AA2 to the starboard fore hull, and AA4 to
the starboard aft hull; their local pivots preserve their intended world pose.
The guns therefore drift with their platforms, while yaw/pitch aiming remains
under native weapon control. Main aiming bones and the main muzzle location
are unchanged. Drift is coded and checked structurally, but has not been viewed
in the game.

The rebuild has 21 bones in both LODs. LOD0: **43,928 vertices / 26,444 triangles**;
LOD1: **17,604 / 11,008**. Both fit the SCM 16-bit index range. Current material
DDS files and their player-color masks are preserved byte for byte. The custom
construction icon, editable OBJ and SCM/DDS previews are regenerated. Combat
blueprint, projectile and brighter detonation behavior are unchanged, including
6,000 damage and 80 range. The prior complete unit and model sources are saved
in `archive/before-naval-hull-2026-10-22/`.

Use `--skip-textures` to retain the existing DDS maps during model iteration;
`--skip-previews` omits the image/icon render stage. Gameplay and installed-copy
verification remain outstanding; no match or game launch was performed.

Materials use `Seraphim` and its native falloff lookup, Albedo.A player-color
lines, SpecTeam reflection/gloss and G/A packed normals. Independent-channel
BC3 mip generation preserves material data beneath zero alpha. All ten mip
levels are decoded for inspection. The construction icon uses normal alpha
processing separately.

The growing charge sphere is a collision-free visual projectile attached to
`MainMuzzle`; it grows for three seconds and hides on firing, target loss,
halt-fire or death. A generation counter invalidates superseded visual threads.
The launched sphere is a separate damaging projectile using the standard
Seraphim experimental phason trail and impact behavior, with enlarged trails.

### Brighter detonation and balance — 22 October 2026

Main damage is reduced from 12,000 to **6,000 per shell**, and maximum range
from 100 to **80**. The 3-second charge and `RateOfFire = 0.1` remain.
`create_impact_effects.py` authors three finite, additive emitters using native
game textures: a white flash (0.30 s particles), a blue ring expanding from
0.6 to 9 size units (0.65 s particles), and outward electrical arcs
(0.45 ± 0.12 s particles). Emitter lifetimes are 0.10–0.15 s. These layers
augment stock phason impacts on units, terrain, props and water; shields receive
the three new layers without ground debris. Native impact processing still
applies damage once. No delayed or secondary damage is added.

Regenerate only these effects with the same Python interpreter:

```powershell
& $tankPython 'mods\seraphim-rework\source-assets\GGA0401\create_impact_effects.py'
```

Sprite/ramp paths and emitter curve names were checked against local FAF
`effects.nx2`, `seraphim_experimental_phasonproj_hit_01/03/06/10_emit.bp`, and
`lua/sim/Projectile.lua`'s impact dispatch. No stock textures were copied.
The previous unit blueprint, projectile script and check record are preserved
in `archive/before-impact-and-balance-2026-10-22/`. Particle timing and brightness
have not been viewed in the game; no match was run for this revision.
